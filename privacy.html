Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 27, 2026

This policy covers the Theo Parallel website and Theo Parallel Uploader (the "App"), a private tool the owner of the Theo Parallel YouTube channel uses to manage the channel's own videos.

YouTube API Services

The App uses YouTube API Services. By using the App, you agree to be bound by the YouTube Terms of Service. Google's handling of your data is described in the Google Privacy Policy.

Who uses the App

The App is used only by the channel owner, who signs in with their own Google account. It is not offered to the public and never accesses anyone else's Google or YouTube account.

What data the App accesses

With the owner's permission, granted through Google's OAuth sign-in screen, the App accesses the owner's YouTube channel to:

  • upload the channel's own videos;
  • set their titles, descriptions, tags, thumbnails, captions, audio tracks and privacy status;
  • read basic information about those videos, such as their ID and processing status.

The App does not read or collect data about viewers, commenters, subscribers or any other YouTube user.

How data is stored and shared

  • The OAuth access token is stored only on the owner's own computer. It is never sent to any server other than Google's.
  • The App has no server and no database, and it does not use analytics or advertising tools.
  • No data is sold, shared with or disclosed to third parties.
  • The owner can delete the stored token at any time by deleting the token file from their computer.

Revoking access

Access can be revoked at any time on the Google security settings page. Once access is revoked, the App can no longer reach the channel.

This website

This website does not use cookies, collect personal data or include tracking scripts. Videos are hosted on YouTube. When you watch them there, YouTube's own terms and the Google Privacy Policy apply.

Children

The App is not directed at children and does not collect information from anyone.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with an updated date.
